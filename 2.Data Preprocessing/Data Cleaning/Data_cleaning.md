### Data cleaning Techniques
(Short overview)
In the general term the cleaning of raw data which contain Null values,duplicacy,redundancy any kind of error in the data and correcting them is consider as the Data cleaning techniques.

### Why we need Data cleaning at the first place?
So,let's imagine a scenario-you are working as the employee in the amazon and u have raw data and now u have to train the model based on that data.
First problem you gonna face is there are for ex:
1.Missing age value 
OR
2.Customer buys shoes then due to error it's showing that in the recent again or in three different rows.
3.One order is $10000000 dollar such a big amount.probably be error or the customer ordered this by mistake.
**These are the reason why we need the Data cleaning techniques**
